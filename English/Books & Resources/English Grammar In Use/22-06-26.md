English Grammar in Use
Unit 2 – Present Simple

Today I studied the Present Simple. I learned that it is used for:

Habits and routines.
General facts.
Scientific facts.
Schedules.
Jobs and permanent situations.

I also learned the difference between the Present Simple and the Present Continuous.

Examples:

I work every day. → Habit.
I'm working now. → Action happening at the moment.

One of the most important things I learned today is that English uses do and does to form negative sentences and questions.

For example:

I work.
I don't work.
He works.
He doesn't work.

At first, this felt strange because in Spanish we only add no, but now I understand that English requires an auxiliary verb.

Present Continuous with "always"

I discovered that always + Present Continuous can express annoyance or frustration.

Examples:

You're always losing your keys.
He's always complaining.

This was a new concept for me because I thought always was only used with the Present Simple.

English Grammar in Use
Unit 4 – Stative Verbs

Today I learned about stative verbs.

These verbs usually describe thoughts, feelings, opinions, possession, or states, so they are not normally used in the Present Continuous.

Some examples are:

know
understand
believe
remember
like
want
need
belong
seem

Examples:

I know him. ✅
I'm knowing him. ❌
The verb "think"

I learned that think has two different meanings.

Opinion

I think you're right.

This uses the Present Simple.

Considering or reflecting

I'm thinking about changing my job.

This uses the Present Continuous.

"Being"

Today I also learned the difference between personality and temporary behavior.

He is selfish.
→ It describes his personality.
He is being selfish.
→ It describes how he is behaving right now.

This was a very interesting concept.

Anki

Today I understood how Anki really works.

At first, I thought I only had to memorize twenty new words every day.

Now I understand that Anki uses spaced repetition to help me remember words over weeks and months.

I also learned that I should mostly use the Good button instead of Easy because a word that feels easy today is not necessarily stored in my long-term memory yet.

Reflection

Today I felt that I understood English grammar much better than before.

The biggest lesson was realizing that English follows its own logic instead of simply translating Spanish grammar.

I also realized that learning grammar, reading books, reviewing vocabulary with Anki, and writing every day complement each other very well.

I believe this combination will help me become more confident in reading, writing, and eventually speaking English.
