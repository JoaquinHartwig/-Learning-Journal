### What I Learned

One of the most interesting concepts today was **variable scope**. I learned that variables declared inside a block only exist within that block, which helps organize code and prevents accidental misuse.

I also learned that braces (`{}`) should always be used with `if` statements, even when there is only one line of code, because omitting them can easily introduce bugs.
